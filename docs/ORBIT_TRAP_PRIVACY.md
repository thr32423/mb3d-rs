# Orbit Trap – privacy

Orbit Trap does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- No account, no sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No network connections: scenes, pictures and settings stay on your iPad,
  in the app's own folder, until you share or delete them yourself.
- Crash reports and TestFlight feedback reach the developer only through
  Apple, if you choose to send them; see Apple's privacy policy.
