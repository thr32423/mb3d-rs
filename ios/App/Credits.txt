Jesse (fractalforums.com)
Wrote the original Mandelbulb3D (2010–2013, Delphi and 32-bit assembly): the distance estimation renderer, Navigator, formula and hybrid system, post processing (shadows, ambient occlusion, depth of field, reflections), Animation maker, Big renders, Monte Carlo renderer. Released it as freeware; his last release was 1.8.9 (April 2013)

Andreas Maschke (thargor6)
Maintainer since 2015: published Jesse's source code on GitHub, moved it to Delphi XE6, released 1.9.0 to 1.99.37 (2023). Added the JIT formula editor, MutaGen, BulbTracer and BulbTracer2 mesh export, the HeightMap generator, ZBuf16Bit, animated maps, the themed interface, point cloud export, Monte Carlo batch and network rendering. His code is under the LGPL 2.1
